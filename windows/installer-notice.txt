SWG Offline lets you play Pre-CU Star Wars Galaxies on this PC with no internet.

It runs a private Core3 server hidden in the background while you play, and saves your galaxy when you quit. The server only listens on this PC (127.0.0.1); nothing outside it can connect.

You need your own copy of the game:
  - an SWGEmu-patched Pre-CU 14.1 client folder, with SWGEmu.exe and its .tre files.
This installer does not include any Star Wars Galaxies files and never copies yours; it only reads them in place.
